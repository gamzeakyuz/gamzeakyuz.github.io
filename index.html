About

Focused on the details most people skip.

I'm an iOS developer based in Istanbul, with a Mobile Technologies degree from Istanbul Okan University and an ongoing Management Information Systems degree at Anadolu University.

My work centers on Swift and SwiftUI, built on MVVM architecture with SwiftData or CloudKit underneath. I care as much about how an interface feels — timing, motion, hierarchy — as I do about how it's structured underneath, and I'd rather ship one well-considered detail than ten generic ones.

Recently that's meant building custom Canvas-based timer dials, WidgetKit and Live Activity integrations, and theme systems designed to hold up across a wide range of visual styles.
